Kosmos surfaces draw their edge and depth with one layered shadow, not a border. In dark mode each shadow adds a dark outer ring and a 1px inner rim of light, so edges still read on a dark page.

Every value below is read from the tokens file, and each surface is shown on the light page and on the dark page.

Every element with a shadow also takes `border border-transparent`. Forced-colors mode drops shadows, and the transparent border then draws the edge.

## Radius

`--radius` sets the control radius, and the rest of the scale steps from it. Tags, badges and pills take `rounded-full`. A surface nested inside another with even padding takes the outer radius minus the padding.
