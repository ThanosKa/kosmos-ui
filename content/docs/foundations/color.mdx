The colors are true grays and ink, with no accent. Kosmos leaves color to status and destructive. They use shadcn's names, so components you install from other shadcn registries pick them up too. `shadcn add @kosmos/tokens` adds them to your global CSS. For other grays, an accent or another radius, pick a preset on the [Presets page](/presets) and take its CSS or registry command.

Each swatch shows the light value on the left and the dark value on the right. Click a swatch to copy its class. This page reads every value below from the tokens file, so it always matches what you install.

## Text

Gray carries the hierarchy. Values stay in `text-foreground`, and idle chrome steps back to `text-muted-foreground`. The current page in navigation takes `text-accent-text`, ink on the flat hover fill. That covers the active sidebar item, the current header tab and the current item in a nav menu. A Meter in its primary tone takes it too. A preset with an accent colors these, focus and the first chart series with it.

## Chart

Chart series take `chart-1` to `chart-5` in order: a mono ramp from ink through four steps of gray. A 1px line in the card color splits stacked segments, so adjacent grays stay apart. Labels and legends stay in text colors.

<ColorTokens group="chart" />

## Tags

A tag marks a category, such as a plan or a segment. Its fill is the `tag-*` color, its text and 1px edge the matching `tag-*-foreground`, which passes 4.5:1 on the fill. Orange, blue, green and red are the status tints of those hues.

<ColorTokens group="tags" />

## Status tints

Six hues mark states and codes: `gray`, `blue`, `green`, `teal`, `orange` and `red`. None of them changes with a preset. A state is a `tint-*-dot` beside its word, through [StatusDot](/docs/components/status#dots). A code, such as an HTTP status, is a [Badge](/docs/components/badge#codes) in the fill and `-foreground`, with no edge. An [Alert](/docs/components/alert) takes the fill, `-border`, `-foreground` and `-link`. Text and links pass 4.5:1 on their fill, and white `tint-solid-foreground` passes 4.5:1 on each `-solid`, in both themes.

The values are steps of [Radix Colors](https://github.com/radix-ui/colors), under the MIT License: `shadcn add @kosmos/tokens` writes its notice into your project as `LICENSE-RADIX-COLORS`.
