## Usage

```tsx
import { DetailDrawer } from '@/components/detail-drawer';

<DetailDrawer
  account={account}
  open={open}
  onOpenChange={setOpen}
  trigger={null}
  onAccountChange={saveAccount}
  onNote={(body) => addNote(account.id, body)}
  onAction={(action, account) => run(action, account)}
/>;
```

Without props the drawer opens from an **Open account** button and shows a sample account. Pass `trigger={null}` and control `open` to open it from a row of the [data table](/docs/blocks/data-table) or a card on the [kanban board](/docs/blocks/kanban-board); the sample account has the same shape as theirs. The `DetailAccount` type and the fields live in the file: change them to fit your records.

## What it does

- **Status** changes in place and calls `onAccountChange` with the updated record. When `account.status` changes from outside, the drawer shows the new status.
- **Notes**: ⌘Enter (Ctrl+Enter on Windows and Linux) or **Add note** adds one. With `onNote`, add the note to `account.activity` yourself; without it, the drawer keeps new notes until another record opens.
- **More menu**: with `onAction`, a menu offers Copy link, Duplicate and Delete account. Ask for confirmation before you delete.
- **Empty fields** say so (_No owner_, _Not scored_), and `loading` shows placeholders.
- **Closing**: Escape, a click outside, a swipe toward the edge or the close button. Focus goes back to the element that opened it.

## Motion

The drawer slides in over 300ms and out in 150ms. With the reduced motion setting on, it fades in and out instead.
