---
title: About blocks
description: Ready-made pieces of a page, such as a sidebar or a settings form, built from Kosmos components.
---

A block is a piece of a page made from several Kosmos components: a sidebar with navigation, a login form, a table with filters. You install it the same way as a component, then change the copy and the data to fit your app.

Each block has a page here, under Blocks, with a live preview and its install command. Blocks lay themselves out by the width they are given, not the screen's, so they work beside a sidebar or inside a panel. The app sidebar is the exception: like the Sidebar, it turns into a drawer on small screens. Each block keeps its sample data at the top of its file, ready to replace. New blocks get an entry in the [changelog](/docs/changelog).
