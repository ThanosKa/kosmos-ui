---
title: About blocks
description: Ready-made pieces of a dashboard, such as a sidebar, a data table or a kanban board, built from Kosmos components.
---

A block is a piece of a page made from several Kosmos components: an app sidebar, a data table, a kanban board. You install it the same way as a component, and it brings the components it is made of. Then change the copy and the data to fit your app.

Blocks lay themselves out by the width they are given, not the screen's, so they work beside a sidebar or inside a panel. The app sidebar is the exception: like the Sidebar, it turns into a drawer on small screens. Each block keeps its sample data at the top of its file, ready to replace, and all of them share the same sample workspace, so they fit together on one page. New blocks get an entry in the [changelog](/docs/changelog).
