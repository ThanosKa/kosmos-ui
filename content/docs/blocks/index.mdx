---
title: About blocks
description: Ready-made pieces of a dashboard, such as a sidebar, a data table or a kanban board, built from Kosmos components.
---

A block is a piece of a page, such as an app sidebar, a data table or a kanban board, that combines Kosmos components. You install it the same way as a component, and it brings the components it uses. Then change the copy and the data to fit your app. Blocks group into block categories by the job they do, such as a data table or a page header. Each category holds one or more variants to pick from.

Blocks lay themselves out by the width of their container, not the screen's, so they work beside a sidebar or inside a panel. The app sidebar is the exception: like the Sidebar, it turns into a drawer on small screens. Each block keeps its sample data at the top of its file, ready to replace. All blocks share the same sample workspace, so they fit together on one page. New blocks get an entry in the [changelog](/docs/changelog).
