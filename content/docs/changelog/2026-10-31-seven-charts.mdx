---
title: Added seven charts
description: Comparison area, Multi-line, Daily stacked bar, Stacked block, Segmented gauge, Candlestick and Flow charts, each with its own motion and keyboard reading.
date: 2026-10-31
---

Seven charts join [Highlight bar chart](/docs/charts/highlight-bar-chart) in the [Charts](/docs/charts) tab. Each reads with the pointer and the arrow keys, repeats its data in a table for screen readers, and moves every mark to its new place when the data changes.

- [Comparison area chart](/docs/charts/compare-area-chart): this period against the one before, with the gap between the two in the tooltip.
- [Multi-line chart](/docs/charts/multi-line-chart): up to five series you can scrub, with a legend that hides a series.
- [Daily stacked bar chart](/docs/charts/daily-stacked-bar-chart): daily totals split by series, each series a stat that hides or shows it.
- [Stacked block chart](/docs/charts/stacked-block-chart): parts of a whole across a few periods as columns of rounded blocks.
- [Segmented gauge](/docs/charts/segmented-gauge): a quota as a half ring of segments that turn destructive past the limit.
- [Candlestick chart](/docs/charts/candlestick-chart): open, high, low and close with faint volume bars.
- [Flow chart](/docs/charts/flow-chart): where things come from and where they go, as bars joined by bands as thick as each flow.
