---
title: Added Pagination
description: Move through a long list a page at a time, pick the rows per page, or load more rows under a list.
date: 2026-10-24
---

- **[Pagination](/docs/components/pagination)**: previous and next buttons, numbered pages that keep the same length on every page and fit a phone, and the rows in view ("21–30 of 2,450") that screen readers hear change. Pages are buttons, or links with `getPageHref`.
- **[Rows per page](/docs/components/pagination#table-footer)**: `PaginationPageSize` changes the page size and keeps the first row of the page in view.
- **[Load more](/docs/components/pagination#load-more)**: `PaginationLoadMore` loads the next rows under a list and says how many are left.
