---
title: Added Radio Group and Number Field
description: Pick one option from a short list as radios or as cards, and change a number with a minus and a plus button.
date: 2026-10-25
---

- [Radio Group](/docs/components/radio-group): picks one option from a short list, as radios beside their labels or as radio cards with a title, descriptions and a price. A selected card's edge turns ink and the circle in its corner fills. Inside a Field it takes its name, description and error from the field, and the arrow keys move the selection.
- [Number Field](/docs/components/number-field): a number between a minus and a plus button, which dim at the ends of the range. The arrow keys step it, Shift takes larger steps, and Home and End jump to the minimum and maximum. `format` writes it as currency, a percent or a unit such as days.
- Field puts a radio on the first line of a label that wraps, as it does a checkbox.
