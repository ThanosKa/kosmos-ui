---
title: Focus stays in view inside ScrollArea and Sidebar
description: A focused control inside a ScrollArea scrolls into view, clear of the edge fade. The Sidebar resize handle keeps its whole focus line.
date: 2026-11-20
---

A [ScrollArea](/docs/components/scroll-area) now scrolls a control into view when it takes focus. This applies when any part of the control sits past an edge or under the edge fade. Before, Chrome left a partly visible control where it was. Tab could then land on a row action or a sortable header with half of it hidden. While `fade` is on, the viewport's scroll padding grows from 4px to 2.5rem on each side. The focused control then stops clear of the fade and keeps its focus ring. This reaches every [Table](/docs/components/table), [Data table](/docs/components/data-table), tab list and scrolling sidebar. With `fade={false}` the padding stays 4px.

The [Sidebar](/docs/components/sidebar) resize handle is 4px shorter at the top and bottom, so the wrapper no longer cuts its focus line.
