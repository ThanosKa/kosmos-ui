---
title: Added Bar list
description: Ranked values, such as top pages or countries, as rows whose fill is the bar, with the value and an optional share at the end.
date: 2026-10-28
---

[Bar list](/docs/components/bar-list) shows ranked values as rows. Each row's fill is its bar, with the label on it and the formatted value at the end. The leader's bar is a step stronger than the rest. It takes muted meta after the label, a share column against the rows or a larger total, and an empty state.
