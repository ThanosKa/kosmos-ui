title: Added the Testimonials block
description: What your customers said, as one quote set large and a wall of cards in columns that move slowly, stop while you read and stand still for those who ask for less motion.
date: 2026-10-25
---

A new block category, Testimonials, which installs with `shadcn add`.

- [Testimonials](/docs/blocks/testimonials): one quote set large under the title, with the person's photo or initials, name and role, then the rest as cards in three, two or one columns by the block's own width. As a wall the columns move up and down at slightly different paces and fade out at the top and bottom. A column glides to a stop while the mouse or a finger is on it, and a button pauses the whole wall. When the visitor asks for less motion, nothing moves and every quote stands in view once. `variant="static"` stands the columns still for everyone.
- The wall moves on the same loop as the [Logo marquee](/docs/blocks/logo-marquee), which ships as `@kosmos/drift` and comes with either block.
