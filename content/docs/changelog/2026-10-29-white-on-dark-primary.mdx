---
title: White text on presets' dark buttons
description: In dark, Soft, Warm, Night and Console fill their buttons with a deeper accent that carries white text.
date: 2026-10-29
---

- **Presets in dark**: Soft, Warm, Night and Console fill their primary controls with a deeper step of their accent, with white text and marks in place of dark ink. That covers the primary button, a checked checkbox or radio, a switch that is on and the slider range. White is 4.5:1 or more on each fill, and each fill stays 3:1 on the page and on cards. The focus ring, the current page in navigation and the first chart series keep their lighter step. Default keeps its black and white. To pick it up, take the preset's CSS or run `shadcn add` for it again.
