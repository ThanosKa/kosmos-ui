---
title: Added Alert, status tints and status dots
description: Six status tints color a new Alert, a dot beside a state and a pill for a code. Orange, blue, green and red tags now use them too.
date: 2026-10-24
---

To pick it up, run `shadcn add` again for `@kosmos/tokens`, then for Badge and Status if you use them. The tokens now also write `LICENSE-RADIX-COLORS` at the root of your project: the tint values are Radix Colors, under the MIT License.

- **[Status tints](/docs/foundations/color#status-tints)**: six hues (gray, blue, green, teal, orange and red). Each has a fill, an edge, text, a link and a dot, plus a solid fill for blue, green, orange and red. Text and links read at 4.5:1 or more on their fill in both themes. They stay the same under every preset.
- **[Alert](/docs/components/alert)**: a message that stays on the page, with an icon, a title, a description and actions. `neutral` is a card. `info`, `success`, `warning` and `destructive` take the blue, green, orange and red tints.
- **[Status](/docs/components/status#dots)**: `StatusDot` shows a state as a dot beside its word, for tables, lists and detail pages.
- **[Badge](/docs/components/badge#codes)**: six `tint-*` variants show a code, such as an HTTP status or a method, in its tint with no edge. In the current item of a nav, blue, green, orange and red fill with their solid.
- **[Badge](/docs/components/badge#tags)**: the `orange`, `blue`, `green` and `red` tags take the tint of their hue, so a blue tag matches info blue. `amber` and `magenta` work the same way. Every tag keeps its edge.
