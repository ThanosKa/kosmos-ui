---
title: Added Banner, changed Alert density
description: A full-width banner for project-wide states, and a more compact Alert.
date: 2026-10-27
---

- **[Banner](/docs/components/banner)**: a strip across the top of an app page. It has a status icon, one sentence and an action, in the neutral card tone or one of four status tints.
- **[Alert](/docs/components/alert)**: padding is 8px by 12px, so one line of text is a 36px row. The icon, the text and the action line up.
- **[Alert](/docs/components/alert)**: a lone description reads at 14px, and an action beside the text sits 4px from the edge.
- **[Alert](/docs/components/alert)**: the tinted variants are subtle by default: a 1px tint edge, tint text and icon, and no fill. `appearance="filled"` brings back the tint fill.
