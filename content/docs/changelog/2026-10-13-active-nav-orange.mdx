---
title: Changed the active sidebar item to orange
description: The current page in the sidebar shows its label and icon in the new active-foreground token, an orange that passes 4.5:1.
date: 2026-10-13
---

The current page now stands out in the brand orange. To pick it up, run `shadcn add` again for each item below.

- **[Tokens](/docs/foundations/color)**: new `active-foreground` color, the text orange for the current page in navigation. It is a darker orange than `ring` in light and equal to `ring` in dark. It passes 4.5:1 on the page, card, popover, secondary and sidebar surfaces and on their hover in both themes.
- **[Sidebar](/docs/components/sidebar)**: the active item keeps its raised surface, and its label and icon turn `active-foreground` instead of ink. `sidebar-primary` no longer colors the active icon.
