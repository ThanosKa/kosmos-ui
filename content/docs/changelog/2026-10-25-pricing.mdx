---
title: Added the Pricing block
description: Plans side by side, with a monthly and yearly switch that screen readers hear, and the featured plan raised on a card.
date: 2026-10-25
---

A new block category, Pricing, which installs with `shadcn add`.

- [Pricing](/docs/blocks/pricing): one to four plans side by side, each with its name, price, a list of features and a link at its foot. When a plan has a price per billing, a switch above the plans picks monthly or yearly, and screen readers hear the new prices once it changes. The featured plan stands on a raised card while the others sit recessed beside it, so it stands out without a color. Names, prices, lists and links line up across the plans, and the plans stack in a narrow container.
