title: Added Sonner

date: 2026-10-29
---

- **[Sonner](/docs/components/sonner)**: call `toast()` from `sonner` and the toasts stack at the bottom of the screen. They spread out on hover, close on a swipe or a quick flick, and pause while the tab is in the background. They take the popover surface, Kosmos buttons and a close button, and only the icon carries color. It is the toast shadcn/ui ships. [Toast](/docs/components/toast) stays for apps built on it.
