title: Added Panel
description: A recessed tray for one section of a dashboard, with a header, the content on a raised card inside it and an optional footer.
date: 2026-10-28
---

[Panel](/docs/components/panel) holds one section of a dashboard. The title, description and actions sit on a recessed tray. The content, a table, a list or a chart, sits on a raised card inside it. A footer on the tray takes pagination or a full-width button. The actions move under the title in a narrow panel, and a long title truncates.
