---
title: Added Dialog, Drawer and Popover
description: The first overlays, for decisions, side panels and anchored details.
date: 2026-10-11
---

Three overlays, each built on Base UI and installed on its own:

- [Dialog](/docs/components/dialog) asks for one decision or one short task. It traps focus, closes on Escape or a click outside, and returns focus to the button that opened it. Its body scrolls once the dialog reaches the height of the screen.
- [Drawer](/docs/components/drawer) slides in from an edge of the screen: a side panel for details, or a bottom sheet on a phone. A side panel is at most 24rem wide and never wider than the container it opens in. It follows a swipe to close.
- [Popover](/docs/components/popover) opens a small panel next to its trigger and flips to the other side when it runs out of room.

Each one has a ready `…Content` part for the common case. It also exports its parts (portal, popup and the like) for layouts of your own. Under reduced motion, all three fade instead of moving.
