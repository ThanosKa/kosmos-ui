- **[Feature rows](/docs/blocks/feature-rows)**: a list of features, one per row, with a title, one sentence and a link beside the media on a recessed stage. The rows alternate sides, and the copy sits above the stage until the block is 768px wide. It lays itself out by its own width. The landing uses it in place of the bento.
