---
title: Added Sidebar, Tabs and Segmented control
description: Three components for moving around an app and switching views.
date: 2026-10-11
---

Three navigation components, each built on Base UI and installed on its own:

- [Sidebar](/docs/components/sidebar): the app sidebar, made of parts you compose. Drag its edge or use the arrow keys to resize it, collapse it to the edge, and on phones it opens as a drawer.
- [Tabs](/docs/components/tabs): switch between panels in one spot, with an underline that slides to the active tab. Rows or columns, and the arrow keys move between tabs.
- [Segmented control](/docs/components/segmented-control): pick one of a few options, like a time range, from a row of segments. Tab lands on the selected item and the arrow keys change it.

Under reduced motion, the tab underline and the sidebar fade instead of moving.
