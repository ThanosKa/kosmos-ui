---
title: Flickering grid settles, and the unread dot holds still
description: Flickering grid stops after 5 seconds, and the Notification inbox unread dot no longer scales under reduced motion.
date: 2026-11-20
---

Two components moved in ways a reader could not stop.

- [Flickering grid](/docs/components/flickering-grid): it flickers for 5 seconds after it first comes into view, then eases to its still pattern and stays still. Under reduced motion it draws the still pattern from the start, as before.
- [Notification inbox](/docs/components/notification-inbox): the unread dot scaled in and out under reduced motion. It now only fades.

To update, run the add command again for each item.
