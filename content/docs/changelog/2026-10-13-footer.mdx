Add Footer with `shadcn add @kosmos/footer`.

- **[Footer](/docs/blocks/footer)**: new block, the free variant of the Footer category in the Marketing section. The product name with one line, columns of links under their titles, and a bottom row with the copyright and icon links. Two columns share a row on a phone, four fit on one row from 576px wide, and from 896px the columns sit beside the product. Pass your router's `Link` as `linkComponent` to move between pages without a full load.
