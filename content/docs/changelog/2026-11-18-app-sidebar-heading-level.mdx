---
title: Added a heading level to App sidebar
description: App sidebar takes headingLevel, so its saved views heading fits under a heading of the page.
date: 2026-11-18
---

[App sidebar](/docs/blocks/app-sidebar) takes `headingLevel` for its **Saved views** heading. It stays an `h2` by default. Pass a deeper level when the sidebar sits under a heading of the page, such as a preview inside a landing section. Screen readers then keep the page's outline.
