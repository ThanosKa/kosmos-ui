---
title: Added the FAQ and CTA blocks
description: Questions that open to their answers with a link to ask more, and a closing section with a two-line title and one action.
date: 2026-10-25
---

Two new block categories, FAQ and CTA, which install with `shadcn add`.

- [FAQ](/docs/blocks/faq): questions under a centered title that open to their answers one at a time, the first one open, with a line under them that links to a contact page. Each question is a heading, Tab and Enter work through the list, and links in an answer read as underlined text. When the visitor asks for less motion, an answer fades in instead of growing.
- [CTA](/docs/blocks/cta): the closing section of a marketing page, a two-line title and one primary action, centered, with wide space above and below. An optional line sits between them.
