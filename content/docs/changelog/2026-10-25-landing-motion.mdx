- **Showcase**: a block that shows your product in a browser-style frame, with a tab for each screen.
- **Text reveal**, **Blur fade** and **Number ticker**: words that come in from a blur, content that fades in once as it scrolls into view, and a number that counts up. All three are plain content without JavaScript and stand still under reduced motion.
- **Flickering grid**: pass `spotlight` and squares near the pointer brighten in a soft halo. It redraws only the squares that changed.
- **Logo marquee**: the logos move from the first paint, before JavaScript loads, and the drift takes over from where they were.
