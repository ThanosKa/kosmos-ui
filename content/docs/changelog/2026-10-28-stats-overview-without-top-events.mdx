---
title: Stats overview can leave out the top events
description: Pass topEvents={false} to show only the metrics and the chart.
date: 2026-10-28
---

- **[Stats overview](/docs/blocks/stats-overview)**: `topEvents={false}` leaves the top events out, so the card ends at the chart. Use it when the page shows other cards below the overview. Nothing changes when you leave the prop out. Run `shadcn add` for it again to pick this up.
