---
title: Added the Navbar block
description: A sticky bar for the top of a marketing page, with dropdowns of links, actions at the end and a sheet on phones.
date: 2026-10-24
---

A new block category, Navbar, which installs with `shadcn add`. The header of this site is built from it.

- [Navbar](/docs/blocks/navbar): your product's name and logo, links, and groups of links that open a dropdown on hover, click or the keyboard, each link with an optional line under it. Buttons sit at the end of the bar. The bar stays at the top of the window on an opaque fill and shows its bottom edge once the page scrolls under it. Below 768px wide the links move into a sheet that opens from the start edge, keeps focus inside, closes with Escape and closes when a link is followed. Pass the address of the page being shown, and its link takes the current-page color.
