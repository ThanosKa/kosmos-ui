---
title: Added Badge, Avatar, Separator, Tooltip and Skeleton
description: Five components that complete the basics next to Button.
date: 2026-10-11
---

Five new components join [Button](/docs/components/button):

- [Badge](/docs/components/badge): a pill for a status, a count or a label, with success, warning and destructive looks that add a colored dot.
- [Avatar](/docs/components/avatar): a person's picture with their initials as the fallback, in three sizes and an overlapping group.
- [Separator](/docs/components/separator): a 1px line between groups of content, horizontal or vertical.
- [Tooltip](/docs/components/tooltip): a short label for a control, shown on hover and on keyboard focus.
- [Skeleton](/docs/components/skeleton): a pulsing placeholder in the shape of the content that is loading.
