---
title: Added Sidebar submenus, workspace switcher and user menu
description: One level of pages under a sidebar item, opening in a popover in icon mode, and the workspace switcher and user menu as Sidebar parts.
date: 2026-10-25
---

- **[Sidebar](/docs/components/sidebar)**: `SidebarSubmenu` holds a level of pages under an item. Beside the page they open in a panel under the trigger. In icon mode they open in a popover beside the icon, on hover, on click or with `Enter`. A trigger that holds the current page takes its highlight while the page is out of sight.
- **Sidebar**: `SidebarWorkspaceSwitcher` and `SidebarUserMenu` put the current workspace in the header and the signed-in user in the footer, each opening a menu. In icon mode they keep their mark or avatar and open the menu beside the column.
- **[App sidebar](/docs/blocks/app-sidebar)**: built on these parts. Events is now a submenu of Live events, Funnels and Alerts, and an item with `items` opens a submenu. A workspace's second line is now `description` instead of `plan`.
