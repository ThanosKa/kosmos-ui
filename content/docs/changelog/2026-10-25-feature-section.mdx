title: Added the Feature section block
description: One feature on a marketing page, shown in a live visual or an image and explained beside it with points that link to more.
date: 2026-10-25
---

A new block category, Feature section, which installs with `shadcn add`.

- [Feature section](/docs/blocks/feature-section): a title over a recessed tray of two cards. The larger card shows the feature, as a live piece of your product or an image; the other explains it with a heading, a short paragraph, a few points that each link to more, and one action. The cards stack on a phone and sit side by side from 768px, and `visualSide="end"` puts the visual after the text so features can alternate down the page. The sample shows a dashboard of active users with a range to switch.
