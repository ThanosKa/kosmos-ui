---
title: Added the Analytics and CRM dashboards
description: Two dashboard blocks built from Panel, Stat card and Bar list, one for traffic and signups and one for deals and quota.
date: 2026-10-28
---

The new [Dashboard](/docs/blocks/dashboard) block category has two variants. Each is a whole page beside the [App sidebar](/docs/blocks/app-sidebar), with every section on its own [Panel](/docs/components/panel) and the KPIs as [Stat cards](/docs/components/stat-card) on one tray. `DashboardAnalyticsPage` and `DashboardCrmPage` bring the sidebar and header; `DashboardAnalytics` and `DashboardCrm` are the content alone, for an app frame you already have.

- **Analytics** (`dashboard-analytics`): traffic against the period before, with a 7, 30 or 90 day switch. Sources, top pages and countries are [Bar lists](/docs/components/bar-list), and the signup goal is a row of meter segments. The newest signups sit in a [Data table](/docs/components/data-table) you can search, sort and filter by plan.
- **CRM** (`dashboard-crm`): the deals with their chance to close as a [Gauge](/docs/components/gauge), in a Data table you can search, sort and filter by stage. It also shows today's next steps, the pipeline by stage, each rep's quota and the team's activity.
