---
title: Changed Kanban board and New item dialog for a product roadmap
description: Kanban board becomes a roadmap with stage columns and cards for area, votes, target and assignee, and New item dialog creates a roadmap item.
date: 2026-10-13
---

Both blocks now show the roadmap of a fictional product analytics app, with new layouts and props. Their props changed, so check your code after you run `shadcn add` again.

- **[Kanban board](/docs/blocks/kanban-board)**: the columns are Ideas, Planned, In progress and Shipped, each with a stage glyph (the new `icon` on `KanbanColumn`). A card shows its area as a tag above the title, then its votes, target quarter and assignee. `KanbanItem` takes `title`, `column`, `area`, `votes`, `assignee` and `target` in place of `name`, `status`, `domain`, `owner`, `arr`, `health` and `lastActivity`. Columns no longer show a revenue total, and each one scrolls in a Scroll area, whose edges fade while cards are hidden past them. The board no longer needs Meter. The sample cards are exported as `sampleRoadmapItems`.
- **[New item dialog](/docs/blocks/new-item-dialog)**: the form makes a roadmap item, with a title and description first, then selects for stage, area, assignee and target quarter. `NewItemValues` is `title`, `description`, `stage`, `area`, `assignee` and `target`. The new `stages`, `areas` and `targets` props set the options. The trigger reads New item, and `NewItemFormValues` and `NewItemStatus` are gone: `values` takes `NewItemValues`.
