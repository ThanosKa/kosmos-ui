title: Added Donut chart
description: Parts of a whole as a ring with the total in its hole, drawn like a Gauge. A legend lists each value and share.
date: 2026-10-28
---

[Donut chart](/docs/charts/donut-chart) shows how a whole splits into parts. Its ring is the ring of [Gauge](/docs/components/gauge): the same stroke, round caps and the same cut between neighboring arcs at every size. The parts take the chart colors in order, a part too small for an arc stays a dot, and hovering a part shows its value and share. It comes in three sizes, with the total and a label in the center, and `DonutChartLegend` lists each part with its value and share.
