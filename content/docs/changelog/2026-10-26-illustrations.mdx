A new item, [Illustrations](/docs/components/illustrations), which installs with `shadcn add`.

- A layer stack that fans out when it scrolls into view or while the pointer is over it. A sync with pulses running between three nodes. A checklist whose rows tick one after the other.
- Each fills the width of its container and scales with it. They pause off screen and stand still for those who ask for less motion.
