---
title: Changed Accordion close timing
description: A closing section shrinks in the time an opening one grows, so swapping sections no longer makes the page bounce.
date: 2026-10-13
---

To pick it up, run `shadcn add` again for the Accordion.

- **[Accordion](/docs/components/accordion)**: a section and its chevron now close in 300ms on the same curve they open with, instead of 150ms. When one section opens as another closes, the content below holds still, or glides once when the sections differ in height. Under reduced motion the closing section collapses at once and the opening one fades in, so the page shifts once instead of twice.
