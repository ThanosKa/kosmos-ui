---
title: Changed Segmented control to slide only to a new selection
description: When the items resize, the raised surface behind the selected item now moves with them instead of sliding after them.
date: 2026-10-31
---

- **[Segmented control](/docs/components/segmented-control)**: the raised surface slides only when the selection changes. When the items change size, such as on a window resize, it stays on the selected item. Before, it slid after the item for 300ms. Run `shadcn add` for it again to pick this up.
