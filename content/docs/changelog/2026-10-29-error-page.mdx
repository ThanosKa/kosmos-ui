- **[Error page](/docs/blocks/error-page)** (`error-page`): a large status code, a title, a line and two actions, centered over a [Flickering grid](/docs/components/flickering-grid). It fills its parent and takes your actions, such as Try again on a 500. This site's own 404 and error pages use it.
