title: Added the Error page
description: A 404 and 500 page and a maintenance page, each over a flickering grid.
date: 2026-10-29
---

- **[Error page](/docs/blocks/error-page)** (`error-page`): a large status code, a title, a line and two actions, centered over a [Flickering grid](/docs/components/flickering-grid). It fills its parent and takes your actions, such as Try again on a 500. This site's own 404 and error pages use it.
- **[Maintenance page](/docs/blocks/error-page#maintenance)** (`maintenance-page`): a countdown to when the app is back and the steps of the upgrade. An optional field collects emails to write to when it ends.
