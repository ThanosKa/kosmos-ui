---
title: Warm buttons take white text
description: In light, Warm's orange fill carries white text, and its focus ring passes on gray surfaces.
date: 2026-10-29
---

- **Warm**: in light the primary button, a checked checkbox or radio, a switch that is on and the slider range keep their bright orange with white text in place of dark ink. White on it is 3.1:1, below the 4.5:1 the other presets meet. The focus ring and the first chart series are a step deeper, so focus now reaches 3:1 on `muted`. Dark mode is unchanged. To pick it up, take the preset's CSS or run `shadcn add` for it again.
