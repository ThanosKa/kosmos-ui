---
title: Added Browser frame and the Hero with product
description: A browser window for screenshots and live screens, and a hero variant that shows a dashboard in it.
date: 2026-10-26
---

- [Browser frame](/docs/components/browser-frame): a window with three neutral dots, an optional address and any content. `tilt` leans it back in perspective and stands it straight on hover, and never moves under reduced motion.
- [Hero with product](/docs/blocks/hero): a title, one line and two actions over a live dashboard in a tilted Browser frame. A Glow sits behind it, and the bottom fades into the page. Swap the screen for an image with `product`.
