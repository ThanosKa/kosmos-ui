---
title: Added Navbar variants
description: A mega menu, centred links, a search trigger and a banner for the Navbar block.
date: 2026-10-26
---

The [Navbar](/docs/blocks/navbar) gains four options, each its own prop. On a phone all of them collapse into the sheet.

- **Mega menu**: an item with `sections` opens a wide panel of titled columns, each link with an icon and a one-line description.
- **Centred links**: `layout="center"` keeps the links in the middle of the bar, whatever the width of the name and the actions.
- **Search**: `search` takes a `NavbarSearch` trigger, which opens your search, such as the command menu. In the sheet it sits at the top.
- **Banner**: `banner` takes a `NavbarBanner`, one line with a link and a dismiss button, above the bar.
