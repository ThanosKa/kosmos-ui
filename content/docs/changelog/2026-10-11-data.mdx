---
title: Added Table, Chart, Meter and Empty state
description: Four components for showing data, and five chart colors in the tokens.
date: 2026-10-11
---

Four new components for dashboards:

- [Table](/docs/components/table): a native table with row hover and selection and aligned numbers. On narrow screens a wide table scrolls inside its own area, with a faded edge and a scrollbar, instead of widening the page.
- [Chart](/docs/components/chart): charts on Recharts. `GrowthChart` shows a value over time, `Sparkline` fits a trend into a stat tile, and the chart parts theme any Recharts chart.
- [Meter](/docs/components/meter): a value within a range, such as a score or a quota, as a segment bar or a continuous bar, colored by a fixed tone or by region.
- [Empty state](/docs/components/empty): what a page, table or panel shows when it has nothing yet, and the step that fills it.

`@kosmos/tokens` now includes `--chart-1` to `--chart-5`, five series colors checked for color-blind readers and for contrast in light and dark. To get them, run `shadcn add @kosmos/tokens` again, or add any of the components above.
