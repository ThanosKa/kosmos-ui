---
title: Added Bento, a grid of live tiles for marketing pages
description: Bento lays out live pieces of your product as tiles, each with one line of caption, under a title.
date: 2026-10-13
---

Add Bento with `shadcn add @kosmos/bento`.

- **[Bento](/docs/blocks/bento)**: new block, the free variant of the Bento category in the Marketing section. Each tile shows a real piece of your product above its title and one line of caption, and a `wide` tile takes two of three columns. The grid lays itself out by its own width: one column, two from 576px and three from 896px. With two columns a lone last tile takes the whole row.
