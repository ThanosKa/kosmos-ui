---
title: Changed Navbar dropdowns to slide between labels
description: Moving from one dropdown to the next slides the links in from that side while the panel resizes.
date: 2026-10-28
---

- **[Navbar](/docs/blocks/navbar)**: moving the pointer or focus from one dropdown label to another slides the old links out and the new ones in from the side of the new label. They move on the same 300ms curve the panel resizes on, and fade with reduced motion. The sample data adds a Resources dropdown, so the demo shows the slide between two dropdowns. The demo sits on a page tall enough to hold an open dropdown. Run `shadcn add` for it again to pick this up.
