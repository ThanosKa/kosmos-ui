---
title: Added Breadcrumb
description: A trail that shows where a page sits and folds the pages that do not fit into a menu.
date: 2026-10-24
---

- **[Breadcrumb](/docs/components/breadcrumb)**: parent pages in grey and the current page in ink. `BreadcrumbTrail` takes the pages as a list and fills the width it is given. When they do not fit, it hides the pages after the first behind an ellipsis that opens them in a menu, and a truncated name shows in full in a tooltip. The parts (`Breadcrumb`, `BreadcrumbList`, `BreadcrumbLink`, `BreadcrumbPage`, `BreadcrumbSeparator`, `BreadcrumbEllipsis`) build a trail by hand.
