---
title: Changed the presets to keep the Kosmos tabs
description: Tabs look the same under every preset, with a line under the active tab in the preset's accent.
date: 2026-10-31
---

- **[Tabs](/docs/components/tabs) under every preset**: the active tab has the Kosmos line under it under every preset, and only its color changes, to the preset's accent. Before, Soft put a tinted pill under the active tab, and Iris a thinner line and a raised view switcher. Console and Night drew the line in another color, Night with a glow. Take the preset's CSS again, or run `shadcn add` for it again, to pick this up.
