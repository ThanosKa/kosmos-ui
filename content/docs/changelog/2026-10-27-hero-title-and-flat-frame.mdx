---
title: Larger hero title, flat hero frame
description: The hero title is 56px, the product frame stays flat, and feature row titles sit a step below section titles.
date: 2026-10-27
---

- **[Tokens](/docs/installation#install-the-tokens)**: `text-hero` is 56px with -0.035em tracking, up from 48px.
- **[Hero with product](/docs/blocks/hero)**: the browser frame no longer leans back, the fade over its bottom half is gone, and the glow sits behind the frame instead of the actions.
- **[Hero with product](/docs/blocks/hero)**: takes an `install` command under the actions, and the line under the title can run 40em wide.
- **[Projects table](/docs/blocks/projects-table)**: below 832px, 672px, 592px and 512px wide it hides Event quota, Plan, Users and MRR in turn, and the Project column narrows on a phone, so the table fits without scrolling sideways.
- **[Feature rows](/docs/blocks/feature-rows)**: row titles are 20px at every width, so they sit clearly below the section title.
