---
title: Added Empty state variants
description: One EmptyState for the five reasons a list is empty, used by the data table, the stat and chart cards, the kanban board, the members list and the usage overview.
date: 2026-10-26
---

- [Empty state](/docs/components/empty) gains `EmptyState`: `first-use`, `no-results` (with the query and Clear filters), `filtered`, `error` (with Try again) and `no-permission`, each with its icon, title and one sentence, all replaceable.
- The data table, stat and chart cards, usage overview, members list and kanban board show it, so an empty column is no longer a bare line of text.
