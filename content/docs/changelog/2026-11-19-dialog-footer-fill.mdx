---
title: Changed Dialog and Drawer footers to a soft fill
description: Dialog and Drawer footers sit on a soft gray fill under their divider, so the actions read as their own row.
date: 2026-11-19
---

[Dialog](/docs/components/dialog) and [Drawer](/docs/components/drawer) footers now fill with `muted` at 50% under the divider, in light and dark, and follow the panel's corner radius under every preset. On a bottom sheet the fill reaches the bottom edge, under the phone's home indicator. Nothing changes in your code: a `DialogFooter` or `DrawerFooter` you already render picks it up when you update the component.
