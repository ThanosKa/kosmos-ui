---
title: Changed Tabs to underline the active tab in its own row
description: When tabs wrap onto more than one row, the indicator now sits under the active tab instead of under the last row.
date: 2026-10-31
---

- **[Tabs](/docs/components/tabs)**: the indicator follows the active tab up and down as well as sideways. Some tab lists wrap onto rows, such as the metric cells of [Stats overview](/docs/blocks/stats-overview) in a narrow container. There, picking a tab in the top row underlined the tab below it. Tabs in one row look the same as before. Run `shadcn add` for Tabs again to pick this up.
