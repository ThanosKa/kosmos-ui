---
title: Pricing changes its prices with a slide
description: The monthly and yearly prices slide and cross-fade, and nothing beside them jumps.
date: 2026-10-28
---

- **[Pricing](/docs/blocks/pricing)**: switching between monthly and yearly no longer drops the old price and blinks the new one in. The old price leaves the way the switch moved and the new one rises in from the other side. The price's width eases, so the period beside it slides, and the period keeps room for its longest text, so the cards keep their height. Props are unchanged. Run `shadcn add` for it again to pick this up.
