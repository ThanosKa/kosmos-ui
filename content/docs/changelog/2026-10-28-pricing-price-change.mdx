---
title: Pricing changes its price digit by digit and shows the yearly saving
description: Only the digits that change slide, and Yearly shows the monthly price struck through, the saving and a discount mark.
date: 2026-10-28
---

- **[Pricing](/docs/blocks/pricing)**: switching between monthly and yearly no longer drops the old price and blinks the new one in. Only the digits that change slide, up when the price rises and down when it falls, and a place the new price no longer has folds away, so nothing beside the price jumps.
- On Yearly, a plan with a price per billing shows its monthly price struck through beside the new one.
- New plan fields: `note`, a muted line under the price (one per billing if you like), and `saving`, shown in green on Yearly only.
- New prop: `yearlyDiscount`, a short green mark such as `−20%` on the Yearly choice of the switch.
- Run `shadcn add` for it again to pick this up.
