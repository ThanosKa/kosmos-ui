---
title: Changed Meter, ScrollArea and Sidebar resize colors
description: The Meter's primary tone no longer matches warning, the scrollbar thumb is lighter, and hovering the sidebar edge stays neutral.
date: 2026-10-13
---

Three color fixes. To pick one up, run `shadcn add` again for `@kosmos/tokens` and the component.

- **[Meter](/docs/components/meter)**: the `primary` tone fills with the new `meter-primary` token, a deeper orange in light mode. Before, `primary` and `warning` had the same lightness and looked alike in grayscale and to color-blind readers. Dark mode keeps the orange it had.
- **[Scroll area](/docs/components/scroll-area)**: the thumb takes the new `scrollbar-thumb` token, lighter than muted text, so the scrollbar no longer outweighs the content. It still has 3:1 contrast against its track.
- **[Sidebar](/docs/components/sidebar)**: hovering or dragging the resize handle shows a neutral grey line instead of an orange one. Orange stays for keyboard focus, so the two never look alike.
