description: Bars that compare periods with one lit and its value in a pill on a dashed line.
date: 2026-10-31
---

[Highlight bar chart](/docs/charts/highlight-bar-chart) compares periods as bars, one of them lit, with its value in a pill at the end of a dashed line. Hover previews a bar, and a click or the arrow keys pin it. New data moves every bar to its new height while the value counts. The entrance plays the first time a chart shows in the browser tab, so a dashboard that people reload all day stays still.
