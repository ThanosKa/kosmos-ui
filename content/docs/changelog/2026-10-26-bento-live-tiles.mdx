---
title: Changed Bento to six live tiles with a link
description: Bento now ships a table, a chart card, a command menu, a range switch, a notification list, and a meter as its sample tiles. Tiles lift on hover and carry a Learn more link.
date: 2026-10-26
---

- **[Bento](/docs/blocks/bento)**: the sample tiles are now live Kosmos components of different sizes. The block installs Table, Chart card, Command, Segmented control, Meter and Badge instead of Field and Switch. A tile's visual lifts on hover, and a new `href` prop (with `linkLabel`) adds a "Learn more" link whose arrow moves with it. The link is the tile's tab stop, and the tile draws the focus outline while it has focus. The grid stays the same: one column on a phone, two from 576px, three from 896px.
