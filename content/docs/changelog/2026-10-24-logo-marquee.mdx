title: Added the Logo marquee block
description: A row of gray logos under one line, still or moving. It has fading edges, a pause button, and no motion for people who ask for less.
date: 2026-10-24
---

A new block category, Logo marquee, which installs with `shadcn add`.

- [Logo marquee](/docs/blocks/logo-marquee): your customers' or your stack's logos in gray, under one line. As a marquee they move toward the start in one row that fades out at both edges. They glide to a stop while the mouse, a finger or keyboard focus is on them. They pause with a button at the end of the row until you press it again. They move only while on screen. When the visitor asks for less motion, nothing moves and the logos stand in centered rows. `variant="static"` sets them in those rows for everyone. Each logo can carry a brand `color` it turns to on hover or focus, and an `href`. On a touch screen they stay gray. Screen readers hear one list, named by the line, with the name of each logo.
