A new block category, Logo marquee, which installs with `shadcn add`.

- [Logo marquee](/docs/blocks/logo-marquee): your customers' or your stack's logos in grey, under one line. As a marquee they move toward the start in one row that fades out at both edges, glide to a stop while the mouse, a finger or keyboard focus is on them, and pause with a button at the end of the row until it is pressed again. They move only while on screen. When the visitor asks for less motion, nothing moves and the logos stand in centered rows. `variant="static"` sets them in those rows for everyone. Screen readers hear one list, named by the line, with the name of each logo.
