To pick it up, run `shadcn add` again for [Segmented control](/docs/components/segmented-control).

- **[Segmented control](/docs/components/segmented-control)**: the raised surface slides to the selected item over 300ms, like the Tabs indicator. Under reduced motion it fades onto the item instead, and it appears in place before the control has been measured.
