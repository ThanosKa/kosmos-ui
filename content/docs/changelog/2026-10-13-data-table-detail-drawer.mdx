---
title: Changed Data table and Detail drawer for an analytics product
description: Data table lists projects with plan and platform filters, totals, and a Pause action. Detail drawer shows one project with its usage and an events trend.
date: 2026-10-13
---

Both blocks now show the projects of a fictional product analytics app, with new columns, fields and props. Their props and types changed, so check your code after you run `shadcn add` again.

- **[Projects table](/docs/blocks/projects-table)**: the rows are projects. Each shows platform, plan as a colored tag, users, events, monthly revenue, an event quota meter, a status dot and the last event. The new `filters` prop takes the plan and platform values of the page header's filters, and `status` takes `live`, `trial` and `paused`, the values of its tabs. The footer totals users, events and revenue. Pause and Resume join the row menu and the bulk bar. The block drops the owner column and the health meter. The new Event quota column replaces them with a meter of the events used against the plan's monthly allowance. `Account`, `AccountStatus` and `sampleAccounts` are now `Project`, `ProjectStatus` and `sampleProjects`. Ticking a row's checkbox no longer opens the row. As the table narrows, Platform and Last event step aside below 1184px of width and Events below 960px. It then fits beside a sidebar before it scrolls sideways.
- **[Detail drawer](/docs/blocks/detail-drawer)**: shows a project's plan, platform and last event, and its users, events and revenue over the last 30 days. It shows the event quota as a meter with the events used out of the plan's allowance. It also shows a line of events per day from the new `trend` field. `account` and `onAccountChange` are now `project` and `onProjectChange`, `DetailAccount` is `DetailProject`, and the more menu offers Copy project ID in place of Duplicate. The body scrolls in a Scroll area, so its edge fades out where content runs past it. The drawer drops the owner, health and labels fields, and it brings Chart, Meter and Scroll area with it.
