---
title: Added the Members block
description: The people in a workspace and the invites on the way, with one form to invite many emails at once.
date: 2026-10-25
---

A new block category, Members, which installs with `shadcn add`.

- [Members](/docs/blocks/members): an invite form that takes a pasted list of emails with one role, marks mistyped and already invited addresses in red, and waits until they are fixed. Below it, the members in a table with a role select in each row and a remove action behind a confirmation, then the pending invites with their state, Resend and Revoke. Each change is announced to screen readers.
