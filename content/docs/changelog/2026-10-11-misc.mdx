---
title: Added Scroll area, Accordion and Asset
description: Three components for scrolling, collapsible sections and media.
date: 2026-10-11
---

Three new components:

- [Scroll area](/docs/components/scroll-area): a scroll container with a thin scrollbar that looks the same on every platform. Edges with hidden content fade out, and the keyboard scrolls it.
- [Accordion](/docs/components/accordion): sections that open and close under their headings. They grow open with a height animation, which becomes a fade when the reduced motion setting is on.
- [Asset](/docs/components/asset): an image or a video in a frame with a fixed aspect ratio. It shows a placeholder while it loads and a fallback if it fails. Videos that play by themselves get a pause button and stay still when the reduced motion setting is on.
