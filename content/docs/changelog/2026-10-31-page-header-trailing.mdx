---
title: Added a trailing slot to Page header
description: Page header takes a `trailing` element at the end of its title row, such as a notification bell.
date: 2026-10-31
---

- **[Page header](/docs/blocks/page-header)**: the new `trailing` prop goes last in the title row, after the More actions menu and the primary action. Use it for a notification bell or an avatar menu. Run `shadcn add` for it again to pick this up.
